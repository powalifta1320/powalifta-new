How the order works
First
Pain, from training or a check-in. Then a meet today or tomorrow, and a busy week waiting for your yes.
Then
A meet inside two weeks, or over their class with one inside eight. A top set a full RPE over what you wrote, or the week half a point over.
Next
A note or a message waiting, a form check, a check-in answer (soreness or stress 8/10 or more, sleep under 6 hours) or 8 days without one.
Last
Five days quiet or a missed session, a block ending with nothing after it, no main lift up in three weeks.

Questions coaches ask

The quickest answer is the demo: twenty lifters on a laptop, no sign-up. Open the coach demo

How do I move my lifters over?

Send each lifter a link. Their past training comes in from a CSV file or pasted rows, and you see every set it read before anything is saved; the same file twice adds nothing. Programs come in pasted, or from a photo or a PDF.

What if my lifters don’t log?

Logging is one tap a set, and it works without signal: sets wait on the phone and sync later. Every set moves their max, PRs and charts, free. Anyone who goes five days without a session lands on your list, with a check-in message ready to send.

How does the price work per lifter?

You pay for a plan sized to your roster, not per head: free up to 3 lifters, Basic up to 10 at $24.99 a month, Pro up to 25 at $49.99, Premium beyond that at $99.99. Lifters use it free.

Who sees my lifters’ data, and can we take it with us?

Each lifter sees all of their own. You see their training, programs, check-ins and notes while they’re on your roster; if they leave, that stops and their history stays theirs. Your payment records are yours alone. Anyone can export everything as a zip of files on any plan, or delete their account. No ads, and no data sold.

Is it on iPhone, Android and the web?

One account works on all three. The iPhone app is for coaches and lifters. On a laptop, sign in at powalifta.com for the full coach desk. On Android it runs in the browser and can sit on the home screen like an app.

Does the app change programs on its own?

No. It suggests next week’s loads and drafts notes and replies. Nothing reaches a lifter until you apply, publish or send it.

Can I write in percentages as well as RPE?

Yes. Write sets the way you do: 3x5 @8, 75%, 2 RIR, RPE ranges, tempo, pauses. Percent sets take their loads from each lifter’s max, starting with the maxes from their intake.

How does the app work out a max?

From the load, the reps and how hard the set felt. Every rep after the first, and every rep left in the tank (10 minus the RPE), counts 3.33% of a max: Arjun’s 182.5 kg × 5 at RPE 8 counts six, so 182.5 is 80% of 228. Try a set of yours.
