Club

Lift with your crew.

Train with your gym or your team, even when you're not in the same room.

  • Most improvedYou against your own best, not against the strongest.

On a laptop

Your coaching desk.

Open POWALIFTA on a laptop and the roster becomes a table, every athlete gets a page, and the program builder types like a spreadsheet.

  • Roster tableBlock week, sessions done, e1RM trend and check-ins for everyone. Sort by who needs you.
