Questions coaches ask

Open the coach demo

What if my lifters don’t log?

Logging is one tap a set, even without signal. Anyone quiet for five days lands on your list, a check-in ready.

Does the app change programs on its own?

No. It suggests loads and drafts notes; nothing reaches a lifter until you publish or send it.

Can I write in percentages as well as RPE?

Yes: 3x5 @8, 75%, 2 RIR, ranges, tempo, pauses. Percentages load from each lifter’s max.

Who sees my lifters’ data?

Each lifter owns theirs; you see it while they’re on your roster. Anyone can export or delete it any time.

Is it on iPhone, Android and the web?

One account on all three: the iPhone app, the full coach desk on a laptop, Android in the browser.

How does the app work out a max?
