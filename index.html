Questions coaches ask first.

All questions

Can I bring my programs over from Google Sheets?

Yes. Paste a block from Sheets or Excel and it becomes a program. 3x5 @8, 75%, 2 RIR and ranges like @7-8 are read the way you write them, and you can check every line before you save. How the switch goes.

Do my lifters pay anything?

No. Every lifter feature is free, forever, on iPhone and the web. You pay for a coach plan once your roster passes three athletes.

Is there an app for my lifters?

Yes, POWALIFTA is on the App Store. It logs offline and syncs when the signal is back. Lifters can also train from any browser.

How is e1RM worked out?

From each set's weight, reps and RPE: the reps left in the tank are counted back in, then the load is read against an RPE chart. The e1RM guide shows the table, so you can check any number.

Can I get my data out?

Any time, on any plan, free included: one zip of CSV files with every set, program and message. What we keep and how to take it.
