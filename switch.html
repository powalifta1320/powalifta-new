Bring your history in.

Years of logs, blocks and meets come with you, so estimated maxes, PRs and progress charts start from day one. All of it goes back out whenever you like.

  • Training logs.Pick a CSV or paste the rows: one row per set with the date, the exercise, weight and reps, and RPE if you have it. A written log works too, like 2 Mar 2026, Squat 180x5 @8.
  • Past blocks.Paste a block the way you write programs (3x5 @8, @7‑8, 2 RIR, 75%) and it's kept with the lifter's past blocks, every set marked done.
  • Meets.Lifters link their OpenPowerlifting profile and every meet comes in, attempt by attempt.
  • Checked before it saves.You see the sets it read, the rows it skipped and why, the dates it covers and the PRs it makes. Import the same file twice and nothing is added twice.

Where to find it

Coaches import for any lifter on their roster: on a laptop, press ⌘K and type “import”; on the phone, You → Your data → Import an athlete's history.

What it counts for

Imported sets count like any other. Estimated maxes, PRs and progress charts fill in from the first day in the file, and percentage work can run from their best estimated max.

Everything goes back out

One zip of CSV files with every set, program and message, on every plan, free included. No contract and no format only we can read. What we keep and how to take it.
