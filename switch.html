Move your coaching over in an afternoon.

Programs in Google Sheets or Excel, results in a group chat, maxes in your head. Here's how coaches bring all of it into POWALIFTA without retyping a set.

  1. Paste your program.Copy the cells from your sheet and paste them in. POWALIFTA reads the way coaches write: 3x5 @8, @7‑8, 2 RIR, 75%, 1x3 @8 then 3x5 160kg. Save it as a template and give it to any athlete.
  2. Send invite links.One link per athlete. They tap it, get the app and land on your roster.
  3. Set the maxes for % work.Percentages work from the max you set for the block, or from each lifter's best estimated max from training.
  4. Bring in their history.Athletes link their OpenPowerlifting profile and every meet comes in with its attempts. Training logs come in from a spreadsheet or CSV too.

Coming from another coaching app?

If it exports your programs to a spreadsheet, paste that in. Same notation, same weeks.

What your athletes get

An app on their phone instead of a shared sheet: today's session ready to go, one tap per set with how it felt, a rest timer, PRs the moment they happen, and meet day planned from what they've actually lifted. Free for lifters, forever.

What you get on a laptop

A roster table that shows who needs you first, a page for every athlete, and a program grid you type into like a spreadsheet, with what they lifted under what you wrote. When the next block starts, the last one is kept, along with what it gained. See the coaching desk.

What stays yours

Your programming and your notation. Any program exports back to a spreadsheet in one click, so nothing is locked in.
